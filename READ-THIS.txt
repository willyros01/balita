THREE FILES. Replace these on GitHub:
  sw.js
  app.js
  version.js

WHAT THIS ADDS
The one condition where the confirmed WebKit bug can actually produce
a wrong result: Wire already running somewhere, AND more than one of
its notifications waiting at once. Under exactly those two conditions,
the newest notification's own text now adds a short note:

  (with others waiting, tapping may open a different one)

WHEN IT DOES NOT APPEAR, ON PURPOSE
On a cold start, since that path doesn't have the bug at all. And when
only one notification is waiting, since there's nothing to guess wrong
between - the correct answer and the fallback answer are the same
thing. Tested directly against all five real combinations before
packaging - the warning only appears in the one case that actually
needs it, every time.

WHY IT HAS TO GO IN THE NOTIFICATION ITSELF
Once the bug actually fires, the app never gets a chance to say
anything at all - that's the whole nature of the bug. This is the
only moment, before any tap happens, where a message can reach you.

WHAT TO DO
Same routine as always - kill the app, delete the icon, add it back
fresh. To see it, get two or more notifications waiting while Wire is
already open somewhere - the note should appear on whichever one
arrives once a second one is already waiting.
