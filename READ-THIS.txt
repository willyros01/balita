THREE FILES. Replace these on GitHub:

WHAT THIS IS
A rebuild, not a patch. The tap now does almost nothing beyond asking
the OS to bring the app forward. No forced navigation, no message
handed across to the page, no repeated attempts, no once-a-second
polling. All of that has been removed.

WHAT REPLACES IT
The destination is written to durable storage the instant a
notification arrives — before it is even shown on screen. Reading it
back is triggered only by the app becoming visible, for any reason:
a tap, opening the icon, or switching back to an already-open tab.
That single moment is the only thing this now depends on, and it is
the one signal that fired reliably in every test run so far,
including the one that otherwise failed completely.

WHAT DID NOT CHANGE
The thirty minute expiry rule and its message. Loading a story
directly if it is not yet in the feed. Everything about how sources,
display and the rest of the app work.

HOW TO TEST
Same as before: delete and re-add the Home Screen icon, send a test
alert from the Actions tab, background the app, tap the notification.
Repeat a few times, including at least one wait past five minutes
before tapping, since that is the case that mattered.

Nothing to read afterward this time — the diagnostic trace has been
removed now that it has done its job. Just tell me whether it opened
the right story.
