WHAT WAS ACTUALLY HAPPENING
Read your report closely and reproduced it directly before touching
anything. When the app comes forward, more than one signal often
fires for that same single moment - this project's traces have shown
that repeatedly throughout the whole investigation. Each of those
signals was being allowed to take its own independent turn. So one
tap could trigger two or three actions back to back, faster than any
of them could be seen - the first one correctly opening what you
actually tapped, and each one after it falling back to the general
queue and opening something else, before you ever got to see the
first result.

Confirmed directly: tapping the newest of three waiting stories, with
two of these signals firing close together, opens the newest one
first - then, an instant later, a second action opens the oldest one
instead, which is the only thing that was ever actually visible.

THE FIX
However many of these signals fire for one real moment of the app
becoming visible, only one actual check now runs for it - the rest
are recognised as the same moment and folded into that single check
rather than each getting their own turn. Proven directly: three rapid
signals now produce exactly one action, while a genuinely separate,
later return to the app still gets its own, completely unaffected.

WHAT TO DO TO SEE IT
Same routine as always - kill the app, delete the icon, add it back
fresh. To actually test this, you'll need three notifications waiting
at once again, the same as before - tap the newest one and confirm it
opens correctly and stays there, then try the others.
