WIRE v0.17.32 — DIAGNOSTIC BUILD

THREE FILES. Replace these on GitHub:
  sw.js
  app.js
  version.js

WHAT IT DOES
Records every step of the notification path — from both the service
worker and the page — into a durable record that survives freezing,
reloading and app updates. Nothing else about the app is changed.

HOW TO READ IT
Open Wire, scroll to the bottom of the story list, to the About panel.
Under the normal About rows there is now a section headed
"Notification trace (temporary)". That is the record.

HOW TO TEST
1. Upload the three files.
2. Delete the Home Screen icon and re-add it, so the new worker is
   definitely the one running.
3. Open Wire, scroll to About, tap "Clear trace".
4. Background the app. Note which screen you left it on.
5. Trigger or wait for a breaking notification.
6. Tap it from the lock screen or Notification Centre.
7. When it lands on the wrong screen, scroll to About and read the
   trace.

WHAT TO SEND BACK
The lines under "Notification trace". Any line starting with EXIT is
the answer — it names exactly where the path stopped.

The four silent exits now each identify themselves:
  EXIT: startup not finished yet
  EXIT: page reports itself hidden
  EXIT: no route found in the cache
  EXIT: already opened this one in this session

The worker lines also show whether navigate was accepted or refused,
which is the other thing that could not be seen from outside.

NOTE
The trace is temporary and will be removed once the fault is found.
