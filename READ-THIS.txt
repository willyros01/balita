WHY THIS TIME IS DIFFERENT FROM GUESSING AGAIN
The logic behind the warning has now been tested twice, correctly,
in isolation - and it still isn't appearing for you on a real device.
Rather than propose a third theory without evidence, this turns the
trace switch back on - the one built specifically so it could come
back without rebuilding anything - and adds one new line that records
exactly what the warning check computes, every single time a push
arrives: how many windows it found, whether it decided the app was
already running, and how many stories it counted as already waiting.

WHAT TO DO
Same routine as always - kill the app, delete the icon, add it back
fresh. In About, tap Clear trace once everything is confirmed at
0.17.53. Then do exactly the test that should show the warning -
Wire already open, backgrounded, and two or more notifications
arriving - and save and send the trace afterward.

WHAT I'M ACTUALLY LOOKING FOR
The new line will say plainly what the check saw at the moment each
notification arrived. If it saw zero windows when the app was
genuinely open, or zero already waiting when there should have been
one, that tells us directly which part of the check isn't seeing what
it should, rather than guessing further from the outside.
