WHAT THE TRACE ACTUALLY SHOWED
Not the WebKit bug this time, at least not the whole story - a real
regression from the previous fix. That fix added a safeguard: once
anything is shown automatically, without a specific tap behind it,
don't let a second such guess immediately follow and overwrite it a
moment later. That part was correct and is still here.

What wasn't right: that safeguard never turned itself back off. Once
it triggered once, it kept blocking every future automatic guess for
the rest of that page's entire lifetime - including a perfectly
legitimate one, minutes later, that should have been allowed through.
That's exactly why nothing opened at all in your last test.

THE FIX
The safeguard now resets itself three seconds after being set. That's
comfortably longer than the near-instant gap the original fix was
built to catch, and short enough that any genuinely later, separate
moment of the app becoming visible gets treated as its own real
question again, with its own real answer - rather than a lasting
"already answered" that quietly refuses everything afterward.

Tested directly before packaging: still correctly blocks a repeat
guess 200 milliseconds later, and correctly allows a fresh one just
over three seconds after that.

Tracing is switched back off in this build, its job for this specific
question done.
