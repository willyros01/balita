WHAT CHANGED FROM v0.17.32
The trace itself had a bug. The worker and the page were sharing one
record, and the page's own once-a-second heartbeat was writing a line
every single tick — including while the app just sits open before any
test even starts. Within about a minute, that flood pushes the
worker's handful of real lines — tap received, route written — clean
out of the record. That is almost certainly why the last trace showed
only page-side lines and nothing from the worker at all.

Two fixes, nothing else touched:
  The worker and the page now keep separate records, so one cannot
  push the other out.
  The heartbeat no longer writes a line when it finds nothing, which
  removes the flood at its source rather than just giving it more
  room.

WHAT I EXPECT TO FIND THIS TIME
If the worker actually runs when you tap a notification, its lines
will now survive and show up first in the merged trace — tap
received, route written, then either navigate accepted or navigate
refused, then focus called.

If there are still no worker lines at all, that is now a real,
solid finding rather than a suspected recording bug: it would mean
iOS is bringing the app forward without ever running the
notification click handler in the worker, which points the fix
somewhere upstream of anything in these files.

HOW TO TEST
1. Upload the three files.
2. Delete the Home Screen icon and re-add it.
3. Open Wire, scroll to About, tap "Clear trace".
4. Background the app.
5. Send a test notification (Actions tab, Fetch news, Run workflow,
   tick the test box).
6. Tap the notification.
7. Open Wire again if it did not already come to the front, tap the
   refresh arrow at the top to redraw the panel, scroll to About.
