WIRE v0.17.45 — TAPPING A SPECIFIC NOTIFICATION NOW OPENS THAT ONE

THREE FILES. Replace these on GitHub:
  sw.js
  app.js
  version.js

THE BUG, EXACTLY AS REPORTED
With two or more notifications sitting on the device at once, tapping
any of them always opened whichever story happened to be oldest in the
queue, not the one actually pressed.

WHY THAT WAS HAPPENING
The queue was built to handle a real problem - the app not being sure
which banner was pressed when it couldn't reliably wake in time to
find out. So it played it safe and always opened the oldest waiting
story instead of guessing. But a tap actually does carry one piece of
certain information: the service worker always knows precisely which
banner was pressed. That certainty was being thrown away in favor of
the same safe guess every time, even when it wasn't needed at all.

THE FIX
The moment a notification is tapped, exactly which one is recorded
separately from the general queue. That specific record now always
takes priority - tapping a banner opens that banner's story, full
stop. The general oldest-first queue still exists, but only comes
into play when there was no specific tap to begin with - opening the
app from its icon, or switching back to it without pressing any
particular notification.

If the specific story that was tapped has expired, or can no longer
be found, that is now what gets reported - not some other story
pulled from the queue instead.

ONE TECHNICAL DETAIL WORTH KNOWING
This required bumping the internal version number of the durable
database itself, separate from Wire's own version number. That is
what makes the new storage actually appear on a device that already
has the app installed from before - tested directly, including
confirming any story you already had waiting survives the upgrade
untouched.

Tested directly before packaging: tapping the newer of two waiting
notifications now correctly opens that one rather than the older one
sitting behind it; tapping an expired one reports it as expired rather
than silently opening something else; and opening the app generally,
with no specific tap involved, still falls back to the oldest waiting
story exactly as before.

HOW TO TEST
Same full routine as always - kill the app, delete the icon, add it
back fresh. To actually see this fixed, you'll need at least two
notifications waiting at once, then tap the one that isn't oldest and
confirm it opens correctly.
